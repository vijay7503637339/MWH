# Maan World Hospitality — Privacy Policy

**Status: Draft — requires client/legal approval before production use.**

Maan World Hospitality may process account details, contact information, profile information, professional information, documents, location information used for attendance, application/job activity, attendance records, earnings/payment records, support requests and device/service information necessary to operate the application.

Information is used to create and manage candidate accounts, match candidates with opportunities, process applications, administer attendance and payments, provide support, maintain security and comply with applicable requirements.

Sensitive documents and payment information must be protected by appropriate technical and organizational controls. Access should be limited to authorized personnel and service providers who need it for legitimate operational purposes.

The application may use notifications and other service communications to provide account, job, attendance, payment and support updates.

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the support details configured by Maan World Hospitality.

This draft must be reviewed and approved by the client and appropriate legal counsel, including applicable retention, deletion, consent, international transfer and grievance requirements, before production release.
